**Overall Territorial Assessment**

* **Fragmented yet hierarchical** – The two macro-cartels maintain a *pyramid* (core families → satellite groups). The core families retain strategic control of **business infiltration** and **financial laundering**; satellite groups handle **street enforcement** (violence, drug sales, extortion).  
* **Geographic concentration of violence** – The **Western** (Bagnoli/Fuorigrotta) and **Central-South** (Quartieri Spagnoli, San Lorenzo) zones experience the **most frequent indiscriminate shootings**, often involving by-standers.  
* **Economic infiltration spreading** – More traditional “street” clans (e.g., **LICCIARDI**, **MAZZARELLA**) are now **investing heavily in legal-sector assets** (construction, health, tourism, fashion) via **pre-titled front companies**, increasing the difficulty of detection.  
* **Emerging “new cartels”** – In **Ponticelli**, the **DE-MICCO-DE-MARTINO** network shows signs of **institutionalization** (formal company fronts, cross-regional money-laundering). In **Pianura**, the **SANTAGATA** group is rapidly gaining violent capability and territorial ambitions.

**Key Tactical Take-aways**

- **Firearms**: Easy access to handguns and Kalashnikov-type rifles; indiscriminate bursts in **Western** and **Central-South** zones increase civilian harm.  
- **Technology**: Use of encrypted messaging, drones, and prison phone infiltration to coordinate logistics.  
- **Financial sophistication**: **Secondigliano Alliance** shows enterprise-level laundering; **Mazzarella** drives **counterfeit-currency** and **hydrocarbons fraud**.  
- **Youth-driven violence**: Many shootings involve young “rampolli” (late teens/early 20s) with high willingness to use firepower.

## 4. Assessment & Strategic Implications  

| Dimension | Assessment (2024) | Implications for Law-Enforcement |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Organizational Structure** | Two **stable macro-cartels** (Secondigliano Alliance, Mazzarella) act as **strategic hubs**; below them a **fluid network** of satellite families with high leadership turnover (arrests, 41-bis comms). | Target **financial nodes** (company fronts, procurement infiltration); focus on **inter-generational leadership links** to disrupt continuity. |
| **Territorial Control** | **Fragmented** but patterned: cores in Centro & Nord (Secondigliano) and East (Mazzarella); volatile hot-spots in West & South-West with rising by-stander exposure. | **Area-specific policing**: high-visibility patrols and intel-led raids in Western and Quartieri Spagnoli; enhance **street lighting/CCTV** in highest-risk streets. |
| **Violence Levels** | **Homicides down** (16→13 province-wide) but **public-place shootings frequent** (Centro, Fuorigrotta). Youth “shoot-for-prestige.” | Expand **firearms tracing**, **ballistics**, **scooter-traffic surveillance**; community outreach to deter minor recruitment. |
| **Drug Trade** | **Core revenue stream**; large seizures at port; **punto spaccio** turf contests drive shootings. | Strengthen **port/customs inspections** (container risk-profiling); broaden intel-sharing on encrypted comms; scale **street-level narcotics teams** in battlegrounds. |
| **Financial Crime** | **Escalating sophistication**: laundering via construction, health, fashion, hydrocarbons fraud, counterfeit notes. | Deploy **financial investigation units** to track front structures, **freeze assets**, and target **professional enablers**. |
| **Cross-regional Links** | **Mazzarella** ↔ **'Ndrangheta (Mancuso, Mazzaferro)**; **Secondigliano** ties to Sicilian Mafia and Lombardy networks via business. | **Inter-regional coordination** (Sicilia, Calabria, Lombardy, Piedmont) for joint investigations and laundering/drug pipeline intel. |
| **Emerging Threats** | **SANTAGATA** (Pianura) and **DE-MICCO-DE-MARTINO** (Ponticelli) show **rapid growth**, potential next-gen cartels. | **Early interventions** to disrupt recruitment; monitor **financial inflows** and violent incidents tied to these actors. |
| **Penal-Institution Infiltration** | Drone smuggling and phone flooding enable **inside-out command**. | **Airspace security audits** at prisons; expand **ECM**; stricter cell-phone bans and monitoring in high-risk facilities. |
| **Community Impact** | High **civilian exposure** to gunfire; growing **youth weapon culture**. | Strengthen **community policing**, youth outreach, and school partnerships to track “scooter-gang” activity. |

### Strategic Recommendations (Priority Order)

1. **Financial Disruption** – Coordinated asset freezes/seizures targeting **Secondigliano corporate networks** (fashion, construction, health) and **Mazzarella hydrocarbons/counterfeit schemes**.  
2. **Targeted Leadership Interdiction** – Leverage **wiretaps** and **41-bis monitoring** to isolate **core family heads** (e.g., CONTINI-BOSTI, MAZZARELLA-D’AMICO) and sever command links to satellites.  
3. **Violence Containment in Western Hot-Spots** – **Rapid-response units** and **CCTV/ANPR** in Bagnoli, Fuorigrotta, Quartieri Spagnoli; surge **evening/weekend patrols**.  
4. **Drug Flow Interdiction** – Intensify **port inspections** (container scanning, risk profiling) and **inter-agency task forces** (GdF, Carabinieri, Polizia di Stato) for cocaine/hash. Expand **street-level narcotics sweeps** in Scampia, Teduccio, Ponticelli.  
5. **Criminal Network Intelligence Fusion** – Stand up a **Naples Criminal Fusion Center** linking police, Carabinieri, Guardia di Finanza, DEA, and customs for real-time intel on **drones**, **encrypted comms**, **inter-regional links**.  
6. **Community & Youth Prevention** – School-based outreach in high-risk quartieri (Fuorigrotta, Pianura); social-service interventions targeting at-risk minors used as foot-soldiers.  
7. **Legal-Procedural Strengthening** – Refine **asset-forfeiture** to hit **pre-titled fronts**; expand **witness protection** for potential *pentiti* from satellite clans.

### Bottom-Line Narrative  

- The Neapolitan criminal ecosystem is **fragmented yet hierarchical**. Two principal cartels orchestrate networks of **satellite clans** fighting over **drug plazas** and **extortion markets**, while **penetrating legitimate sectors** to launder profits.  
- **Violence is endemic**: homicides trend modestly down, but **public-space shootings** remain frequent, especially in **Western** and **Central-South** districts.  
- **Drug trafficking** remains the **revenue backbone**, with large imports via the port and contested **spaccio** driving acute rivalries.  
- **Financial sophistication** is rising: **Secondigliano** excels at **enterprise-level infiltration**; **Mazzarella** leads **counterfeit-currency** and **hydrocarbons fraud**.  
- **Emerging groups** (**SANTAGATA**, **DE-MICCO-DE-MARTINO**) are building next-gen **cartel structures** that could shift the balance if not deterred early.

**Immediate focus**: **cut financial lifelines**, **disrupt command channels**, and **contain high-impact violence** in densely populated districts via a **multi-agency, intelligence-led, community-oriented** approach.
